We're always happy to hear from reliable people who take pride in their work and enjoy being part of a small local team.

We have opportunities across residential cleaning, Airbnb turnovers, move in and move out cleans, and new build cleaning.

About the role

The work varies from day to day and can include residential homes, Airbnb turnovers, move in and move out cleans, and new build cleaning.

You'll often be working independently or as part of a small team, moving between different properties around Wānaka.

Attention to detail, reliability and good communication are really important to us.

What matters to us

  • You show up when you say you will
  • You notice the little things
  • You're comfortable working independently and as part of a small team
  • You communicate clearly if something isn't right
  • You're happy moving between different properties during the day
  • You have a valid NZ driver licence and reliable transport
  • Cleaning experience is helpful, but reliability and a good attitude matter just as much

We're also open to hearing from experienced independent contractors, as well as people looking for casual or part-time work.

What the work involves

Cleaning work can be physically active and may include vacuuming, mopping, stairs, repetitive tasks, carrying linen bags and moving between properties during the day.

The role may also include weekend, public holiday or short-notice work depending on bookings and client needs.
