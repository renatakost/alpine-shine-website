Airbnb Turnover Cleaning

Guest-ready, every time.

When a guest walks through the door, the little things matter. A beautifully made bed, a spotless bathroom, a fresh kitchen and a home that feels ready for their stay all make a difference.

Our Airbnb turnover cleaning service in Wānaka is for owners and hosts who want their property consistently cleaned, reset and presented to a high standard between bookings.

We take care of the clean, the presentation and a final check of the property before we leave.

What’s included

Every property is different, but a typical turnover includes:

  • Full property clean between bookings
  • Bathrooms cleaned and sanitised
  • Kitchen surfaces, sinks and appliances cleaned
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Beds made and presented for incoming guests
  • Fresh hire linen and towels arranged where required
  • Living areas, cushions and furnishings reset
  • Rubbish and recycling removed
  • Client-supplied guest essentials restocked
  • Final presentation check
  • Visible damage or maintenance issues reported

More than a regular clean

An Airbnb turnover is a little different from cleaning a home that someone lives in every day.

We are preparing the property for a new guest, so we also look at how everything is presented and whether something feels out of place.

If we notice damage, a maintenance issue or something that needs your attention, we let you know.

Linen Hire

Need linen for your holiday home?

We can arrange professionally laundered hire linen as part of your turnover, including bed linen and towels, so you do not have to manage your own washing between stays.

Guest Consumables

If you supply guest essentials such as toilet paper, tea, coffee and other consumables, we can restock them during the turnover.

If you would prefer us to manage consumables for you, we can also discuss an ongoing arrangement.
