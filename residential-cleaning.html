Residential Cleaning

Reliable home cleaning that fits around your life.

Whether you need regular cleaning or a one-off refresh, we help keep your home clean, comfortable and easy to maintain.

Every home is different, so we work around what your property actually needs rather than using the same checklist for everyone.

Regular Cleaning

Ideal for weekly, fortnightly or recurring cleans.

A typical clean may include:

  • Bathrooms cleaned and sanitised
  • Kitchen surfaces and exterior appliances cleaned
  • Dusting of accessible surfaces
  • Vacuuming carpets and rugs
  • Mopping hard floors
  • Mirrors cleaned
  • Bins emptied and liners replaced
  • High-touch areas wiped down
  • General tidy and reset of the home

Deep Cleaning

Sometimes a home needs more than a regular maintenance clean.

Our deep cleaning service is for homes that need extra time and attention. This might be a seasonal clean, a home that has not been professionally cleaned for a while, or simply a more thorough reset.

Deep cleaning can include extra attention to areas such as:

  • Skirting boards
  • Doors and door frames
  • Detailed bathroom cleaning
  • Kitchen surfaces and cabinetry exteriors
  • Build-up in harder-to-reach areas
  • Detailed dusting
  • Additional time on floors and surfaces
  • Spot cleaning of marks on walls where suitable
  • Other areas agreed with you before the clean

Optional Add-ons

Some jobs need a little extra time and can be added separately when required.

Full Oven Clean
Available as an additional service on request.
Interior Windows

Wall Cleaning

If there is anything else you would like us to focus on, we can discuss it with you before the clean.

Regular or One-off

You do not need to commit to a weekly clean.

We can help with regular ongoing cleaning, fortnightly visits or a one-off clean when your home needs some extra attention.

Tell us about your home

Tell us a little about your home, what you would like help with and how often you are looking for a clean. We can then work out what suits your property best.
