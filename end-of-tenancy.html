Move In & Move Out Cleaning

A fresh start for the next chapter.

Moving is already a lot to organise. Whether you are leaving a rental, selling your home, moving into a new property or preparing a house for someone new, we can take care of the cleaning so there is one less thing on your list.

Our move in and move out cleaning is a more detailed clean designed for properties that are empty, or almost empty, and need to be thoroughly cleaned before the next person moves in.

When this service can help

This service is suitable for End of Tenancy Cleaning, homes being prepared for sale, properties that have just been purchased, or simply when you want a proper clean before moving in or handing the keys over.

What’s usually included

Every property is different, but a typical move in or move out clean may include:

  • Bathrooms thoroughly cleaned and sanitised
  • Kitchen surfaces, sinks and exterior appliances cleaned
  • Cupboards and drawers wiped where accessible and empty
  • Skirting boards, doors and door frames cleaned
  • Dusting of accessible surfaces
  • Mirrors cleaned
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Built-up dust and marks cleaned where reasonably possible
  • Bins emptied
  • General detailed clean throughout the property
  • Spot cleaning of marks on walls where suitable
  • Final check before we leave

A more detailed clean

A move in or move out clean usually needs more time than a regular residential clean.

Once furniture and belongings have been removed, there are often areas that have not been easy to reach during everyday cleaning.

We work through the property carefully and focus on getting it ready for whoever comes next.

Optional Add-ons

Some tasks require extra time and can be added separately when needed.

Full Oven Clean
Available as an additional service on request.
Interior Windows
Available as an additional service on request.
Wall Cleaning
Full wall cleaning can be added when required and is quoted separately depending on the size and condition of the walls.

If there are other areas that need extra attention, let us know when you request your quote and we can discuss what is needed.

Before we arrive

For the best result, we recommend having as many personal belongings and loose items removed from the property as possible before the clean.

If the property will still contain furniture or belongings, just let us know so we can work around what is there.

Tell us about the property

Whether you are moving in, moving out, selling, buying or reaching the end of a tenancy, tell us a little about the property and when you need it ready.

We can then work out what the clean will involve and provide a quote based on what is needed.
